About AROHA

Empowering Indian college graduates and job seekers with data-backed skill intelligence.

Product Vision & Philosophy

Traditional career guidance relies on outdated academic syllabi or anecdotal advice. AROHA bridges the academia-industry gap by converting raw Indian job-market signals into actionable, forward-looking skill intelligence.

"Don't just prepare for today's jobs — prepare for tomorrow's skills."

RESPONSIBLE USE

Known limitations

AROHA supports decisions; it does not replace human judgement. Salary relationships are observational. Frontier signals are projections because the supplied market snapshot does not contain reliable longitudinal dates. Model results should be interpreted with their sample sizes and validation metrics.

Platform Architecture & Standards

Platform: AROHA Enterprise Workforce Intelligence
Edition: v1.0.0 Production Release
Architecture: Python 3.12, FastAPI, SQLite, Scikit-learn, Vanilla CSS Glassmorphism, Chart.js
Data Notice: Uses a structured, 1,800+ record verified benchmark dataset representing major tech hubs (Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, Gurugram, Mumbai). Forecast values are clearly labeled as statistical momentum projections.
