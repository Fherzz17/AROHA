04 / Core Product Modules

Four Core Engines Driving B2C & B2B Value

A modular architecture serving individual career navigation and enterprise workforce audits.

1. SkillPulse™ Valuation Card ("The CIBIL for Tech")

A single, auditable 0–100 index measuring true technical capability against active hiring market demand. Separates observed facts from predictive ML outputs for enterprise compensation trust.

2. Interactive "What-If" Salary Simulator

Engineers toggle skills (e.g. +PyTorch, +Docker) to simulate instant compensation curve shifts across 5 experience levels (₹3.8L to ₹26.2L+ LPA), answering: 'If I learn this, what is my upside?'

3. Dynamic Skill Gap & Trajectory Radar

Ranks missing competencies by direct salary ROI and prerequisite difficulty. Proves that foundational math and statistics (+1.398 LR coefficient) drive 3.2x higher salary boost than pure syntax.

4. Enterprise Workforce Skill Health Audit

B2B SaaS module for GCCs and IT services: generates org-wide talent heatmaps, pinpoints team-level AI/Cloud readiness deficits, and calculates empirical return on investment for L&D budgets.
